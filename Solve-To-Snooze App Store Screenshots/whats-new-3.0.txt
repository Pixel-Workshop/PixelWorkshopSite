Solve To Snooze 3.1 — a whole new look, and a smarter way to wake up.

A BRAND-NEW DESIGN
• Rebuilt from the ground up with Liquid Glass for iOS 26.
• The alarm screen now has a sky that brightens toward dawn as you work through your challenge, and breaks into a sunrise when you solve it.
