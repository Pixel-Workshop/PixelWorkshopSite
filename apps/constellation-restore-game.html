Constellation Restore Game

Reconnect scattered stars, restore full constellations, then take them into the real sky. Version 2.0 adds Find in Sky with a live camera overlay and guidance arrow, a richer Constellation Atlas, Tonight's Sky, 3-star puzzle ratings, Peek Mode, and discovery cards that turn each solve into a deeper astronomy moment.

Download on the App Store

Requires iOS 17 or later. Find in Sky requires camera and location access and works best on a physical device.

Features

Restore It, Then Find It

📷

Find in Sky

Point your camera at the night sky and see a real-time overlay that draws constellation lines over the stars, with a direction arrow when the target is out of frame.

📚

Constellation Atlas

Unlock mythology, featured star facts, and sky guides as you solve, all collected in a growing atlas with detailed constellation views.

🌙

Tonight's Sky

See which constellation is featured tonight, why it is worth finding, your observing streak, and jump straight into the real-sky finder.

⭐

Star Ratings

Earn up to 3 stars on each puzzle based on how few hints you use, with your best rating saved per constellation.

👁️

Peek Mode

Hold the peek button to briefly reveal the solution shape, letting you study the pattern without fully giving up on the puzzle.

✨

Discovery Cards

After each solve, a full-screen discovery card celebrates the constellation with mythology, star facts, and observing guidance before adding it to your atlas.
