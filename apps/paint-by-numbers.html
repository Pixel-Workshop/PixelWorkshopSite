Paint by Numbers

Paint by Numbers is a calming color-by-number puzzle app designed for short, satisfying creative sessions. Tap numbered cells to fill them with curated colors, complete intricate designs, unlock new palettes, take on daily art challenges, and build a personal gallery of finished works — all without ads, timers, or pressure.

Creative Without the Clutter

Tap-to-Paint Controls

Select a color, tap numbered cells to fill them in. Simple, precise, and satisfying with every tap.

Curated Color Palettes

Every design ships with a hand-picked palette that makes the finished piece look great without color theory knowledge.

Daily Art Challenges

A new design drops every day. Complete it to keep your streak alive and unlock bonus palettes over time.

Growing Design Library

Browse categories like animals, landscapes, abstract, and seasonal themes with new packs added regularly.

Personal Gallery

Every completed painting is saved to your gallery. Revisit, share, or just admire your collection as it grows.

Hints When You Need Them

Stuck on a tricky section? Use hints to highlight remaining cells for the active color so you never lose momentum.

Zoom and Pan

Pinch to zoom into detailed areas and pan around the canvas for precise painting on even the most complex designs.

Fully Offline

All downloaded designs work without an internet connection. Paint anywhere, anytime — no connectivity required.
