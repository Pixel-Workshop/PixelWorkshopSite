Refract

Refract is a laser puzzle game built around clear logic and satisfying feedback. Route beams through mirrors and prisms, play with ambient music and haptics, switch on accessible beam palettes and symbols, track your progress with Game Center, and jump into the daily challenge from iPhone, iPad, or the Home Screen widget.

Features

Light, Logic, and Feel

Prisms and Splitters

Break a single beam into multiple color paths and route each one to its own destination.

Mirror Routing

Rotate reflectors and redirectors to thread light through tight, deliberate puzzle layouts.

Handcrafted Levels

Every stage is built by hand to teach one new mechanic at a time and then combine it with others.

Ambient Sound and Feedback

Play with an ambient music loop, interaction sound effects, and haptics for placement, rotation, removal, and puzzle completion.

Color Logic

Mix, split, and separate beams as puzzle rules grow more layered and more satisfying to solve.

Independent Settings Controls

Turn music, sound effects, and haptics on or off independently from a dedicated settings screen.

New Features

Accessibility, Competition, and Daily Play

Color Blind Modes

Switch to accessibility palettes for Deuteranopia, Protanopia, or Tritanopia and keep puzzles readable without relying on default colors.

Symbol Overlay

Turn on optional beam symbols so shapes carry the puzzle information alongside color.

Game Center Achievements

Unlock milestones for stars earned, puzzles solved, login streaks, and hint-free clears.

Leaderboards and Sync

Compete on global leaderboards for total stars and login streak while keeping your Game Center profile synced to progress.

Native iPad Layout

Play with a full iPad layout and sidebar navigation designed to make longer sessions feel spacious and readable.

Daily Challenge Widget

Keep today's challenge on your Home Screen and deep link straight into the puzzle from the widget.

Design

A puzzle game about clarity.

Refract is designed to feel readable from the first second. When a board gets harder, it should be because the logic is deeper, not because the interface is noisy.

The goal is short, satisfying puzzles that reward experimentation. New sound, haptics, accessibility options, and wider device support are there to make that clarity hold up whether you are on iPhone, iPad, or opening the daily challenge from the widget.
