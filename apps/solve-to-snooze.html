Solve To Snooze

Make mornings harder to ignore and easier to manage. Wake up with math, typed phrase, barcode scan, or step-count challenges, test alarm readiness in the Reliability Check Center, schedule exact one-time or repeating alarms with faster presets, and track your wake score, streaks, snoozes, and recent alarm history in one place.

Features

Harder To Ignore, Easier To Run

🧩

Wake-Up Challenges

Choose the challenge that gets you moving: math, typed phrase, barcode scan, or step count.

🛡️

Reliability Check Center

Test alarm access, sound, challenge flow, and lock-screen readiness before tomorrow morning does it for you.

⏰

Exact One-Time Alarms

Schedule one-off alarms precisely when you need them, alongside repeating routines for the rest of the week.

⚡

Quick Presets

Set Weekdays, Weekends, Every Day, Tomorrow Only, or Skip Next in a couple of taps instead of rebuilding schedules by hand.

📈

Escalation Mode

Repeated snoozes can make the next alarm tougher, helping you break out of the loop when a normal snooze cycle is not enough.

🚫

Real Max-Snooze Limits

Set a true cap on how many times you can snooze and let the app enforce it cleanly when the limit is reached.

💊

Routine Reminders

Use challenge alarms for medication, hydration, or start-of-day routines where a normal notification is too easy to ignore.
