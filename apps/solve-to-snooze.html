Solve To Snooze

A whole new look, and a smarter way to wake up. Rebuilt from the ground up with Liquid Glass for iOS 26, Solve To Snooze makes you prove you're awake with a memory pattern, math, typed phrase, barcode scan, or step-count challenge while the sky brightens toward dawn. Solve it and the screen breaks into a sunrise. Earn your sleep.

Features

Harder To Ignore, Easier To Run

Wake-Up Challenges

Choose the challenge that gets you moving: memory pattern, math, typed phrase, barcode scan, or step count, each at Easy, Medium, or Hard.

Reliability Check

Run a quick checklist for alarm access, sound, a practice challenge, and a lock-screen test before tomorrow morning does it for you.

Exact One-Time Alarms

Schedule one-off alarms precisely when you need them, alongside repeating routines for the rest of the week.

Quick Presets

Set Weekdays, Weekends, Every Day, Tomorrow Only, or Skip Next in a couple of taps instead of rebuilding schedules by hand.

Escalation Mode

Repeated snoozes turn the alarm ember red and switch to a harder challenge right away, helping you break out of the snooze loop.

Real Max-Snooze Limits

Set a true cap on how many times you can snooze and let the app enforce it cleanly when the limit is reached.

Routine Reminders

Use challenge alarms for medication, hydration, or start-of-day routines where a normal notification is too easy to ignore.

Daily Flow

See What's Next and How You're Doing

Today, Tomorrow, Later, and Off

Alarms are grouped by when they ring, with disabled alarms tucked into Off, so your schedule is easy to scan at a glance.

Next Up Countdown

See your next alarm immediately with a live countdown on a card tinted to the time of day it rings.

Insights Dashboard

Track your on-time streak, wake score, average snoozes, hardest solve, a 7-day trend, and recent wake-ups.

Sounds and Challenges, Side by Side

Pick from Rise, Siren, Pulse, Blare, March, or Chime, tap to preview, and set the dismiss challenge and difficulty on the same screen.

Instant Scheduling Alerts

If an alarm can't be scheduled, you'll see it right away, with a one-tap Retry.

Private by Design

Everything stays on your device. No account, no cloud dependency, no ads, and no subscriptions.
