How It Works

Simple process.
No surprises.

We keep things straightforward. You always know where your project stands and what it costs.

01

Discovery call

We start with a 30-minute call to understand your idea, your users, and what success looks like. No commitment required — just a conversation.

02

Scope & proposal

We send you a written proposal with a fixed scope, fixed price, and a realistic timeline. No hourly billing, no scope creep — what we quote is what you pay.

03

Build

We build your app using our AI-assisted workflow. You get regular updates and early builds to test. Feedback is welcome at every stage.

04

Launch & handoff

We submit to the App Store, handle the review process, and hand off the full codebase. You own everything, no strings attached.
