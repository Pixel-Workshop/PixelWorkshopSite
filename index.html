How we work

Software should earn its place on your home screen.

That means a clear purpose, a fair business model, and an interface that does not fight for more attention than it deserves.

More about Pixel Workshop →
  1. 01

  3. 03

    No recurring fee where a recurring service does not exist.

  4. 04
