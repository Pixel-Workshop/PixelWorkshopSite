Privacy Policy

Constellation Restore Game

Last updated: April 2026

Overview

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Constellation Restore Game ("the App"), developed by Pixel Workshop LLC ("we," "us," or "our"), handles your information. The short version: we do not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Information We Do Not Collect

Constellation Restore Game does not collect any personally identifiable information. Specifically, the App does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or any contact information
  • Your location data sent to us
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or crash reports sent to us
  • Any gameplay statistics, observing streaks, or progress data sent to us

Data Stored on Your Device

All data generated by Constellation Restore Game — including your puzzle progress, atlas unlocks, star ratings, observing streak, and settings — is stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

If you delete the App, all locally stored data will be removed from your device.

Camera and Location Permissions

Find in Sky can request camera and location access so the app can show a live sky overlay from your current position. Camera access is used to display constellation lines over your camera view, and location access is used to calculate where the constellation appears from where you are.

Camera imagery and location data are processed on-device for this feature. We do not collect, store, or transmit your camera feed or your location to Pixel Workshop servers.

Offline Gameplay

Core puzzle play, atlas viewing, and saved progress can be used offline. Find in Sky relies on device permissions and works best on a physical device, but it does not require an account with Pixel Workshop.

Third-Party Services

Constellation Restore Game does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The App does not send your puzzle progress, atlas data, camera feed, or location to Pixel Workshop servers.

Children's Privacy

Constellation Restore Game does not collect data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The App complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) by not collecting any personal information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our support page.
