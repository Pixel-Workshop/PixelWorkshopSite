Privacy Policy

Match Mint

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Match Mint ("the App"), developed by Pixel Workshop LLC ("we," "us," or "our"), handles your information. The short version: the App itself collects no personal data about you, we never sell or share your information, and the only personal data we ever receive is what you choose to put in a support message.

Information We Do Not Collect

Match Mint does not collect any personally identifiable information. Specifically, the App does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or any contact information
  • Your location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or crash reports sent to us
  • Gameplay activity tied to your identity

Data Stored on Your Device

All data generated by Match Mint — including high scores, daily challenge results, streak reward progress, settings, and preferences — is stored locally on your device, and is not accessible to Pixel Workshop. The exception is Game Center: if you are signed in, eligible scores are submitted to Apple's leaderboards as described below.

If you delete the App, all locally stored data will be removed from your device.

Offline Gameplay

Match Mint is designed so core gameplay works offline. Standard play, local progress, and most score tracking do not require internet connectivity.

Third-Party Services

Match Mint does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The App does integrate Apple Game Center for campaign and daily leaderboards. When you use Game Center features, Apple may process related data according to Apple's own privacy policies.

Game Center and Network Use

Game Center leaderboards and profile sync may require internet connectivity and Apple's services to update. We do not receive your Apple ID credentials, and we do not use Game Center data for advertising or tracking.

In-App Purchases

Match Mint offers optional in-app purchases. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store, and the App uses Apple's StoreKit framework to check which items you own and to restore them on your devices. Those checks require a network connection to Apple. We never receive or store your payment card details, billing address, or Apple Account credentials.

Our Website and Contact Form

This section describes the Pixel Workshop website rather than the App. When you submit the contact form on a support page, your name, email address, selected topic, and message are transmitted to Formspree, a third-party form-processing service in the United States, which delivers them to us. We use that information only to answer you and keep it no longer than needed to resolve your request. Please do not include sensitive personal information, or private content from the App, in a support message.

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with most web hosting, standard server logs, including visitor IP addresses, are recorded by the host to serve the site and protect it from abuse. Our pages also load typefaces from Google Fonts, which discloses your IP address to Google when a page loads. We do not use advertising cookies, and we run no analytics on the website.

Your Privacy Rights in the EEA and the UK

Pixel Workshop LLC is the data controller for the limited processing described in this policy. Because Match Mint keeps your content on your device and we operate no account system and no server of our own, there is normally no personal data held by us to access, correct, or erase. Where we do process personal data, principally a support message you choose to send us, our legal basis is our legitimate interest in responding to you.

Subject to the conditions set out in the GDPR and the UK GDPR, you have the right to request access to personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, to receive it in a portable form, and to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. To exercise any of these rights, contact us through our support page. Information you send us is processed in the United States.

California Privacy Rights

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We have not done so in the preceding twelve months, and we do not use or disclose sensitive personal information beyond the purposes permitted without a right to limit.

The only category of personal information we receive is the identifiers and message contents you choose to send us through our support form. California residents have the right to know what personal information we hold, to request its correction or deletion, and not to be treated differently for exercising those rights. Submit a request through our support page; we may need to verify that the request comes from you.

Children's Privacy

Match Mint is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We do not use persistent identifiers to build profiles, to serve advertising, or to track users across apps or websites. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through our support form, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our support page.
