Privacy Policy

Paint by Numbers

Last updated: May 2026

Overview

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Paint by Numbers ("the App"), developed by Pixel Workshop LLC ("we," "us," or "our"), handles your information. The short version: we do not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Information We Do Not Collect

Paint by Numbers does not collect any personally identifiable information. Specifically, the App does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or any contact information
  • Your location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or crash reports sent to us
  • Painting activity or design choices tied to your identity

Data Stored on Your Device

All data generated by Paint by Numbers — including completed paintings, in-progress work, gallery data, daily challenge streaks, unlocked palettes, and preferences — is stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

If you delete the App, all locally stored data will be removed from your device.

Offline Functionality

Paint by Numbers is designed so that core gameplay works offline once designs are downloaded. Painting, saving, and gallery browsing do not require internet connectivity.

Third-Party Services

Paint by Numbers does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The App does not use Game Center or any social platform integrations.

Network Use

An internet connection may be used to download new design packs or check daily challenge availability. No personal data is transmitted during these requests.

Children's Privacy

Paint by Numbers does not collect data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The App complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) by not collecting any personal information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our support page.
