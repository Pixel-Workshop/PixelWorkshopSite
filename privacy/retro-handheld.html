Privacy Policy

Retro Handheld

Last updated: April 2026

Overview

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Retro Handheld ("the App"), developed by Pixel Workshop LLC ("we," "us," or "our"), handles your information. The short version: we do not collect, store, or share your personal data, and the app is designed to keep gameplay local to your device.

Information We Do Not Collect

Retro Handheld does not collect any personally identifiable information. Specifically, the App does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or any contact information
  • Your location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or crash reports sent to us
  • Any information about the game files you import or play

Data Stored on Your Device

All data generated by Retro Handheld — including imported game files, save data, save states, cheat codes, controller mappings, settings, and preferences — is stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

If you delete the App, all locally stored data will be removed from your device.

Imported Game Files

Any game files you import into the App, including ROMs or disc images, are stored locally on your device. We have no access to these files at any time. You are solely responsible for ensuring you have the legal right to use any files you import.

Third-Party Services

Retro Handheld does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The App does not require an internet connection for normal gameplay, and its bundled open-source emulation cores run locally on your device.

Children's Privacy

Retro Handheld does not collect data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The App complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) by not collecting any personal information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our support page.
