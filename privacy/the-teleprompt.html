Privacy Policy

The Teleprompt

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

The Teleprompt ("the App") is developed by Pixel Workshop LLC ("we," "us," or "our"). This policy explains how the App handles your scripts, recordings, purchases, and support requests. The short version: we do not collect your personal data, your scripts and recordings are yours, and nothing is sent to a server we operate.

Scripts and Settings

Your scripts, folders, and app settings are stored on your device. When iCloud is available, the App uses your private iCloud database to synchronize scripts and folders between devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Those synchronized records are stored by Apple's iCloud service and are not accessible to us. Some app settings may remain specific to a single device.

The App does not send your scripts to a server operated by Pixel Workshop. You can delete scripts and folders at any time inside the App. Deleting a folder leaves its scripts available outside that folder. Changes to synchronized records depend on iCloud availability and sync timing.

Camera, Microphone, and Recordings

The Teleprompt requests access to your camera and microphone so you can record video and audio. Recordings are processed on your device. The App requests add-only permission for Photos when you choose to save a take.

The App does not upload your recordings to any service operated by us. If you share a recording with another app or service, that service's own privacy practices apply. If iCloud Photos is enabled on your device, recordings you save may sync through Apple's Photos service according to your settings.

Deleting a script does not delete a recording you have already saved to Photos or shared elsewhere. Manage saved recordings in Photos and in any service you sent them to.

Purchases

Apple processes The Teleprompt Pro purchases. The App uses StoreKit to check whether the Pro upgrade is available to you and to restore it on your devices. We do not receive your payment card details through the App.

Advertising and Tracking

The Teleprompt does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs. It does not require an account with us, and it does not ask for your name, email address, location, or advertising identifiers. Apple may process service, transaction, or diagnostic information under its own policies and your device settings.

Support Requests

If you contact us through the support page, we receive the information you choose to include and use it only to respond to you. Please do not send script text or recordings unless they are needed to explain your issue and you want to share them.

Your Choices

You can manage camera, microphone, Photos, and iCloud access for The Teleprompt in your device settings at any time. You can use the prompting and rehearsal tools without ever recording. Syncing requires iCloud availability; local use requires no account at all. Deleting the App removes its locally stored data from your device.

Our Website and Contact Form

This section describes the Pixel Workshop website rather than the App. When you submit the contact form on a support page, your name, email address, selected topic, and message are transmitted to Formspree, a third-party form-processing service in the United States, which delivers them to us. We use that information only to answer you and keep it no longer than needed to resolve your request. Please do not include sensitive personal information, or private content from the App, in a support message.

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with most web hosting, standard server logs, including visitor IP addresses, are recorded by the host to serve the site and protect it from abuse. Our pages also load typefaces from Google Fonts, which discloses your IP address to Google when a page loads. We do not use advertising cookies, and we run no analytics on the website.

Your Privacy Rights in the EEA and the UK

Pixel Workshop LLC is the data controller for the limited processing described in this policy. Because The Teleprompt keeps your content on your device and we operate no account system and no server of our own, there is normally no personal data held by us to access, correct, or erase. Where we do process personal data, principally a support message you choose to send us, our legal basis is our legitimate interest in responding to you.

Subject to the conditions set out in the GDPR and the UK GDPR, you have the right to request access to personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, to receive it in a portable form, and to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. To exercise any of these rights, contact us through our support page. Information you send us is processed in the United States.

California Privacy Rights

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We have not done so in the preceding twelve months, and we do not use or disclose sensitive personal information beyond the purposes permitted without a right to limit.

The only category of personal information we receive is the identifiers and message contents you choose to send us through our support form. California residents have the right to know what personal information we hold, to request its correction or deletion, and not to be treated differently for exercising those rights. Submit a request through our support page; we may need to verify that the request comes from you.

Children's Privacy

The Teleprompt is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We do not use persistent identifiers to build profiles, to serve advertising, or to track users across apps or websites. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through our support form, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App changes. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our support page.
