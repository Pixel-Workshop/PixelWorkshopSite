Camera access lets the app draw the constellation overlay over the real sky, and location access is used to calculate where the constellation appears from where you are. These permissions are used on-device for the feature and work best on a physical device running iOS 17 or later.
Solving a constellation can unlock a discovery card with mythology, featured star facts, and sky guidance. That content is then saved into the Constellation Atlas so you can revisit it later.
