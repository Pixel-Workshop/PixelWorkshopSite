FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Choose a design from the library, select a color from the palette at the bottom, then tap the numbered cells that match that color to fill them in. Complete all cells to finish the painting.
A new design is available every day. Complete it to keep your daily streak alive. Consecutive completions can unlock bonus color palettes and exclusive designs over time.
Tap the hint button while painting to highlight all remaining cells for the currently selected color. This helps you find cells you may have missed, especially in complex designs.
Yes. Pinch to zoom in on detailed sections and drag to pan around the canvas. This makes it easy to paint precisely on even the most intricate designs.
Finished paintings are automatically saved to your personal gallery within the app. You can revisit them at any time. All gallery data is stored locally on your device.
Yes. Your progress on any design is automatically saved. You can close the app and pick up exactly where you left off at any time.
Paint by Numbers stores all data locally on your device. If you restored from a full iOS backup, your progress should carry over. Otherwise, previous progress may not be recoverable.
Core gameplay works fully offline once designs are downloaded. An internet connection may be needed to download new design packs or sync daily challenge availability.
Use the contact form below and include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened. Steps to reproduce the issue are especially helpful.
Paint by Numbers does not collect personal data or analytics for Pixel Workshop. All data stays on your device. See the Paint by Numbers Privacy Policy for full details.
