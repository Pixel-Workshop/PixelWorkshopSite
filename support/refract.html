Open the Settings tab in Refract. Sound effects and haptics each have their own toggle. Refract plays no music of its own — its chimes are mixed so that whatever you are already listening to keeps playing.
Yes. Turn on Colorblind assist in the Settings tab and every beam colour is given its own distinct shape, so no puzzle information depends on colour alone. Refract also supports VoiceOver, Dynamic Type and Reduce Motion.
Finishing a board earns the first star, solving it within the move budget earns the second, and a clean run — inside par plus two, with no hints and without clearing the board — earns the third. Taking a mirror back off the grid is always free and never counts against you. Hints come in two stages: the first marks the cell, the second places the mirror, and only the first one charges.
