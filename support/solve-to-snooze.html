First, make sure Notifications are enabled in iPhone Settings → Notifications → Solve To Snooze, and confirm your alarm is toggled on inside the app. Then open the Reliability Check Center and run the alarm access, sound, challenge flow, and lock-screen readiness tests to catch anything that could block the next alarm.
Solve To Snooze supports math problems, typed phrase, barcode scan, and step-count challenges. You can choose the wake-up method that fits the kind of push you need in the alarm editor.
Barcode Scan needs camera access, and Step Count needs Motion & Fitness access. Check those permissions in iPhone Settings for Solve To Snooze. For step count, also make sure you are carrying the phone while taking the required steps.
The Reliability Check Center is a built-in test area for alarm access, sound playback, challenge flow, and lock-screen readiness. It is there to help you confirm the app is ready before you depend on it the next morning.
